About IEEE Student Branch

About Us

The IEEE Student Branch at PET Engineering College is a newly established student initiative focused on creating a platform for students to explore technology, innovation and professional development.

The branch aims to bring students together through technical workshops, guest lectures, hands-on sessions, project showcases and other learning activities.

Our goal is to encourage students to learn emerging technologies, develop practical skills, share ideas and actively participate in the IEEE community.

  Our Objectives

  • Conduct technical workshops and hands-on sessions
  • Organize guest lectures and technical talks
  • Encourage project development and innovation
  • Develop technical and professional skills
  • Promote teamwork, creativity and knowledge sharing

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide students with opportunities to learn emerging technologies, develop practical and professional skills, and participate in technical activities that encourage innovation and collaboration.

Our Vision

To build an active and innovative student community where students learn, create, collaborate and contribute to technological development.
